WHY WE BUILT THIS

The register was never the problem. Counting it by hand was.

Most small and medium supermarkets still run on paper registers or scattered spreadsheets. Stock counts drift from reality, bills get miscalculated, and reports take an evening to put together — every single day.

SmartMart replaces all of that with one simple, always-current view: add stock, record sales, and let the system handle the arithmetic and the analytics.

Built as a minor project by Group 47, Department of Computer Engineering, R. C. Technical Institute, Ahmedabad — on Python, Streamlit, and MySQL.
