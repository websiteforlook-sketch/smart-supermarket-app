WHAT IT DOES

Everything a small shop actually needs — nothing it doesn't

No modules to configure, no menus to get lost in. Four screens cover the whole day: what's in stock, what sold, what's running low, and what to hand your accountant.

📦

Live stock tracking

Every sale and restock updates the count immediately — know what's in and out, down to the unit, without a manual recount.

🔍

Barcode ready

Plug in any USB scanner. Scan an item to restock it instantly, or add it as a new product in seconds if it's not in the system yet.

📊

Clear dashboards

Top sellers, stock by category, and low-stock alerts on one screen — see what's moving without digging through spreadsheets.

🧾

One-tap sales entry

Pick a product, set the quantity, done. Stock and totals update together, so the books never fall out of sync.

📄

Bulk import

Already have a product list in Excel or CSV? Upload it once and every item lands in the catalogue, matched by column name.

⬇

Export anytime

A clean, styled Excel workbook with your full catalogue and sales history — ready to print, file, or send along.

GETTING STARTED

From signup to your first sale in four steps

The setup follows the order a real shop actually runs in — set the shop up, load what's on the shelves, then start ringing things through.

01

Set up your shop

Enter your shop name and owner details once — you're straight into the dashboard, no separate login step.

02

Load your catalogue

Add products one by one, or bulk-import an existing spreadsheet in a single upload.

03

Scan & sell

Scan a barcode to restock, or record a sale in two taps — stock adjusts itself either way.

04

Review & export

Check the dashboard for what's low or what's selling, then export a report whenever you need one.
